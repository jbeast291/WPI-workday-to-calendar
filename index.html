WPI Workday to Calendar Export your calendar from workday into a format Outlook/Google/Apple calendars can import.
DISCLAIMER: This tool is made by students. Always double-check your exported calendar for inaccuracies. This tool provides no warranty.

Step 1: Get your .xlsx file from Workday

1Go to Workday
→
2Click "Academics"
→
3Click "View My Courses" (on the right)
→
4Click (Top right of schedule)
→
5Save the .xlsx for the next step

Step 2: Choose your .xlsx file below

This tool works fully within your browser (your schedule does not leave this device)
